# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chinese job seekers who want to turn existing experience, resumes, project notes, and job descriptions into polished, role-specific resumes. They may be students, interns, career switchers, or working professionals, and they need a guided tool that keeps their source materials organized while helping them tailor each application quickly.

## Product Purpose

This product is an AI-powered Chinese resume optimization tool. Users build a reusable personal profile and experience library, analyze a target JD, generate a structured resume from real user-provided material, preview it in fixed professional templates, edit sections, save versions, and export PDF, Word, or TXT files. Success means users can trust that the AI never fabricates experience and that the final resume looks consistent across preview and export.

## Brand Personality

Professional, precise, restrained. The interface should feel like a serious job-search workspace: calm, direct, and confident, with clear steps and visible controls.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS landing-page styling, colorful gradients, vague AI magic copy, hidden navigation, template choices that only change colors, and preview screens that look like plain text editors instead of real resumes.

## Design Principles

1. Source truth first: personal profile and experience library are the durable source, generated resumes are editable outputs.
2. Template means structure: choosing a template changes layout, section order, hierarchy, and export composition, not only color.
3. Make every step reversible: users should always see where they are, return home, go back to the previous step, and edit before exporting.
4. Trust beats flourish: AI output must expose gaps, avoid invention, and preserve user control over sections and wording.
5. Preview is the product: the resume preview should resemble the final document, with the same modules and structure used for export.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear Chinese labels, high-contrast black and white UI, keyboard-accessible forms and buttons, visible disabled states, and predictable navigation. Avoid relying on color alone for state, and keep browser-print PDF as the primary Chinese-safe PDF path.
